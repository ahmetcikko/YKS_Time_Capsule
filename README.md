# YKS Time Capsule

## What this project is

I am currently in my exam year, the college entrance exam i will be entering is YKS (Turkiyes national university entrance exam, like the SAT or ACT) and i noticed that i wasnt properly keeping track of my scores in mock exams and how my exam prep was evolving. I didnt want to use loads of notebooks and papers keeping track of how my prep is going so i decided to make it into a digital app. Its not finished yet, ive barely even started however it is under development and will be ready soon.
